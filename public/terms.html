These are the terms for using WeGoPlace, run by Imaginative Prompting. By making an account you agree to them. What we keep and why is on the privacy page.

Who can use it

You need to be at least 13, or older if the law where you live says so for using a service like this on your own. One account is for one person. Buying the plan needs someone 18 or over, or of age where they live if that's older.

Your account

Keep your passkeys, and any Google or GitHub sign-in you use, to yourself. What's done with your account is yours to answer for, including what an AI app you connect does with it.

What WeGoPlace is

A place for a group to plan a trip together. It's free. An optional plan, for someone who hosts trips, adds more room for photos and videos and the assistant. It's still being built: what it does may change, it has limits on how much a trip and an account can hold and how fast they can be used, and it may sometimes be down.

A plan in WeGoPlace isn't a booking. Times, opening hours, prices and directions can be wrong or out of date, whoever wrote them, an AI app or the assistant included. Check what matters with the airline, the hotel or the place itself.

Your content

What you put in WeGoPlace stays yours. You let us keep it, copy it to the devices of the people on your trip, and show it to them, and to anyone who has the link to a board you share, for as long as it's there, only to run WeGoPlace. That permission ends when you delete it, apart from the copies the others' devices already hold and the database's restore points, which go within 30 days.

Put in only what you have the right to share. What you add to a trip is seen by everyone on it.

Trips and the people on them

A trip's host can remove people from it and hand it to someone else on it. If you leave a trip or delete your account, what you paid and owed stays in its Split, and what you added to its shared plan stays on it.

AI apps and the assistant

An AI app you connect, and the assistant where the site offers it, act as you, on every trip you're on, within what you let them do. Every change they make is on the trip's list of changes, and anyone on the trip can undo it. What they write can be wrong: read it before you rely on it.

The plan

The plan is optional, and everything else in WeGoPlace stays free for everyone. It belongs to one account and gives the trips that account hosts two things: room for photos and videos, 50 GB across the trips you host in place of 1 GB, and the assistant for everyone on those trips. It's bought by the account that hosts the trips; nobody else on them pays. These terms cover the plan as they cover the rest, and also say:

Price and renewal: $5 a month or $48 a year, plus tax where it applies. It renews at the end of each month or year and charges the payment method you gave, until you cancel. If the price changes, we'll write to the email you gave when you paid at least 30 days before it applies to you, and you can cancel before it does.

Canceling: any time, on Your account. The plan keeps working until the end of what you've paid for, then stops. Payments aren't refunded for part of a month or year, except as below or where the law requires it.

Changed your mind: within 14 days of first buying the plan, write to us and we'll refund that payment in full. The plan ends when it's refunded.

When the plan ends (canceled, a payment that can't be taken, or a refund): nothing is removed. Every photo and video stays, and everyone on the trip can still open and download it. If the trips you host hold more than the free 1 GB, no new photos, videos or other files can be added to them until they fit again or the plan is back (plans, places, purchases and chat still work), and the assistant stops answering. When a payment fails, Stripe tries again for a while, and the plan keeps working meanwhile.

A disputed payment: while your bank looks into a payment you disputed, the assistant is paused on your trips. If the dispute goes your way, the plan ends.

The assistant: a share each month for everyone on the trips you host, back on the 1st (UTC); what's left doesn't carry over. When the month's share is used up, the assistant stops answering until the next month. It runs through OpenRouter, on a model we choose and may change. It's for questions about a trip and changes to it: don't use it for anything these terms don't allow, or to get around its limits. Like any AI, it makes mistakes.

Changes to the plan: a change that gives you less applies only from a renewal at least 30 days after we write to the email you gave when you paid, and you can cancel before it does.
