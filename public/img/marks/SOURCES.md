# Other people's marks

Each is its owner's, used only on the button that signs in with that owner, as the owner asks.
Neither is this project's to license.

- `google-g.png`: Google's "G", the file from Google's Sign in with Google branding guidelines
  (developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines, fetched 13 Nov 2026), unchanged. The
  guidelines' rules for a custom button, as read that day: the button must carry the standard
  color G; its size and colors can't be changed; it sits on white (outline #747775, text
  #1F1F1F), on #131314 (outline #8E918F, text #E3E3E3) or on #F2F2F2; an older G is not
  allowed; the words are "Sign in with Google", "Sign up with Google" or "Continue with
  Google"; the button is at least as prominent as any other provider's. `public/css/account.css`
  (`.lg-google`) follows those. Where the page departs: the words are set in the app's own
  font at the app's button size, not Google Sans Medium 14/20.
- GitHub's mark is not a file: it is drawn inline in `login.html` and `account.html` from
  `mark-github-16.svg` in GitHub's Octicons (github.com/primer/octicons, MIT), unchanged, in
  black on a light theme and white on a dark one (github.com/logos: the mark appears in black
  or white, is not recolored or reshaped, and may be used to show that a product works with
  GitHub).
